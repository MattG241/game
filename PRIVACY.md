# Lunar Cleanup - Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 October 2026_

Lunar Cleanup ("the game") is a single-player game. It has no accounts, no sign-in and
no servers of its own. This policy explains what data is involved when you play.

## Data the game keeps on your device

- **Save games, settings and crash breadcrumbs** are stored only in the game's private
  storage on your phone. They are never uploaded. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
- The **"Copy crash log"** button copies a diagnostic report to your clipboard. Nothing is
  sent anywhere unless you choose to paste and share it yourself.

## Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads that you choose to watch
for an in-game bonus, and occasional full-screen ads between play sessions. To serve and
measure ads, Google's ads SDK may collect and process:

- your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
- approximate location derived from your IP address,
- device information (model, operating system, language) and app interaction data
  (for example, which ads were shown and whether they were tapped),
- diagnostic data used to prevent fraud and keep the SDK working.

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by the developer of Lunar Cleanup.
See how Google uses information from apps that use its services:
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and Google's privacy policy:
<https://policies.google.com/privacy>.

**Your choices**

- In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland (and other regions where it is
  required) the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using
  Google's consent form. You can change your choice at any time in
  **Menu > GAME > AD PRIVACY OPTIONS**.
- You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ads personalisation, in
  your phone's **Settings > Google > Ads** (or **Settings > Privacy > Ads**).
- Without an internet connection no ads are loaded and the game plays normally.

## Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The optional **Remove ads** purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. The developer
never sees your payment details. The game only receives confirmation that the purchase
was made, so it can turn the full-screen ads off. See Google Play's terms and privacy
policy for how Google processes payments.

## Children

Lunar Cleanup is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly
collect personal information from children.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with a
new date. For questions, contact the developer at the email address shown on the game's
Google Play store listing.
